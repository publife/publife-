How It Works

Kansas City Metropolitan Area
A simple, transparent process designed to help hospitality workers receive the care they need.

What PubLife Covers

PubLife provides fast, efficient, and confidential financial assistance to hospitality workers in our community. This includes emergency room visits, urgent care, pharmacy costs, mental health services, and substance abuse treatment.

Stewardship & Accountability

PubLife is built to operate as a true nonprofit, with a strong focus on directing the majority of funds toward those in need. Our model targets allocating 80% to 85% of all funds raised directly to medical grants while keeping operating costs low and intentional.

How Funds Are Distributed

When an application is approved, funds are distributed directly to the provider or institution associated with the medical expense whenever possible. This helps ensure assistance is used as intended.

Oversight & Fairness

PubLife operates as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with oversight from a Board of Directors. Applications are reviewed using established criteria and handled with consistency, fairness, and respect.

Our Commitment

At its core, PubLife exists to respond when it matters most by providing timely, dignified support to the people who keep our communities running every day.
