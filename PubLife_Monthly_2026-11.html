November 2026

A Message From The Founder Of PubLife

November has been another busy month for PubLife. As we continue to grow, we're finding that building an organization and operating one are two very different things. Both take work, and we're learning something new at every step.


Over the past several weeks, we've continued developing the programs, partnerships, and operating systems that will allow PubLife to serve our hospitality community more effectively. Some of that work happens out in the community, while quite a bit happens behind the scenes. Either way, it's all part of building something that can last.


One of the things I'm most proud of is seeing PubLife become more than an idea. We're developing the tools and relationships necessary to connect people with assistance, strengthen our industry, and create a more sustainable future for the organization.


There's still plenty of work ahead, but we're moving in the right direction. Thank you to everyone who continues to support us, encourage us, and believe in what we're building.


Together, we're serving the ones who serve us.


With gratitude,


Vincent Mandacina

Founder & Executive Director


If You Missed It...

The Shared Impact Program Is Ready to Launch


One of our biggest milestones this fall is the completion of the Shared Impact Program's development phase. The program brings employees and participating businesses together around a common mission: supporting PubLife.

Through the program, employees will have the option to contribute small amounts from each paycheck to support PubLife's mission. Participating employers will then have the opportunity to match those contributions, helping create a more sustainable source of funding for our work.


We're excited to move from development toward implementation and begin putting the program to work. It's another important step in expanding our fundraising initiatives so we can continue supporting the community.


Building PubLife for the Long Term


We've also made significant progress developing automated systems to reduce time-consuming administrative work and create more opportunities for community outreach and fundraising.


This work is about more than making day-to-day administration easier. We're building the infrastructure necessary to keep PubLife organized, accountable, and sustainable long after its founding and early development. We want to build an organization that can continue serving hospitality workers for generations, not one that depends on a handful of people holding everything together.


Improving How We Provide Assistance

With every assistance grant we process, we've continued refining the systems and procedures that support our work. We've strengthened our application workflow, privacy safeguards, documentation procedures, and internal recordkeeping.


These improvements help us handle applications more consistently, protect sensitive information, and keep the focus where it belongs: getting people connected with the assistance and resources they need.


A lot of this work isn't necessarily visible from the outside, but it's essential to building an organization that people can depend on.

Partner Spotlight

THE CRAIC ON MAIN

This month, we're shining a spotlight on The Craic on Main in historic downtown Parkville!


Located on Main Street, The Craic is a neighborhood Irish pub built around good food, good company, live music, and a welcoming atmosphere. Owned by Pat and Ginger O'Reilly, the pub has become a gathering place where friends, families, and neighbors can come together and enjoy a little bit of Irish hospitality.


Now, we should probably disclose that we're not entirely unbiased here. Owner Pat O'Reilly also serves on PubLife's Board of Directors. But even if he didn't, we'd still have plenty of reasons to sing their praises. Their Reuben is outstanding, and their smashburger is something of a hidden gem. We're talking about one of the best smashburgers for miles around!

The word craic is an Irish expression for fun, good conversation, and enjoyable company. It's a fitting name for a place that values connection and community.


We're grateful to Pat, Ginger, and the entire team at The Craic for being part of the local hospitality community and for supporting the mission of PubLife.


If you're in Parkville, stop by, enjoy a meal, and see what the craic is all about!


Visit The Craic on Main at 12 Main Street in Parkville, Missouri, or learn more at craiconmain.com.

Get Involved

Help Us Build What Comes Next


PubLife has made meaningful progress, but the work is far from finished. To continue providing assistance, launch new programs, and build an organization that will be here for the long term, we need continued support from our community.


We need donors. We need business partners. We need people willing to help spread the word.


Whether you make a one-time contribution, become a recurring supporter, introduce us to a business that might want to get involved, or simply share our mission with someone in your network, you are helping PubLife reach more people.


Recurring donations are especially valuable because they give us a more dependable foundation to plan ahead and respond when someone needs help.


If you believe the people who take care of our community deserve someone looking out for them, we invite you to be part of this mission.


Visit publife.org to learn more, make a contribution, or get involved. You can also reach us at info@publife.org.


Every connection matters. Every contribution helps. And every person who helps spread our mission brings us one step closer to serving more of the people who serve us.
