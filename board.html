BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Our current board of directors has been carefully selected to represent multiple walks of life with different experiences, opinions and points of view to ensure that we not only govern PubLife fairly but represent different corners of the community. All have ties to the hospitality industry and all hold a strong respect for the workers who serve us everyday.
